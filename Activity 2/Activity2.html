Stranger Things

Set in the 1980s in the town of Hawkins, Indiana, Stranger Things follows a group of kids who uncover government secrets, supernatural forces, and a mysterious girl named Eleven, as they try to find their missing friend Will.

Key Information

  • Genre: Science Fiction, Horror, Drama
  • Created by: The Duffer Brothers
  • First Released: July 15, 2016
  • Platform: Netflix

Cast & Creative Team

  • Millie Bobby Brown as Eleven
  • Finn Wolfhard as Mike Wheeler
  • David Harbour as Jim Hopper
  • Winona Ryder as Joyce Byers
  • Produced by: Shawn Levy, Dan Cohen

Main Characters

  • Eleven – A girl with psychokinetic abilities.
  • Mike Wheeler – Leader of the friend group.
  • Jim Hopper – The town's police chief.
  • Will Byers – The boy who mysteriously disappears.
  • Joyce Byers – Will’s determined mother.
